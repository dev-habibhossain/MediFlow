3. Patient Accounts & Security

To schedule consultations or access digital prescriptions, you must register a patient account. You agree to:

  • Provide accurate, current, and complete legal personal and insurance information.
  • Maintain the security and confidentiality of your login credentials and password.
  • Promptly notify MediFlow of any unauthorized access or security breach involving your account.

4. Appointment Booking & Cancellation Rules

Our real-time scheduling system operates under strict operational parameters to ensure clinical punctuality for all patients:

A. Real-Time Slot Reservation

When you confirm an appointment online, that physician's time slot is reserved exclusively for you.

B. Rescheduling & Cancellations

Appointments may be rescheduled or cancelled free of charge up to 2 hours prior to the scheduled consultation time via your patient portal.

C. Late Cancellations & No-Shows

Cancellations made less than 2 hours before the appointment or failing to attend a scheduled consultation without notice may result in a nominal late-cancellation fee billed to your account.

5. Telehealth Consultations

Virtual appointments conducted via MediFlow's encrypted video portal are subject to the following conditions:

  • You must be physically located in the jurisdiction where your consulting physician is licensed to practice at the time of the call.
  • Telehealth is suitable for non-emergency follow-ups and routine consultations; the physician reserves the right to request an in-person physical exam if clinically necessary.

6. Fees & Insurance Billing

Consultation fees are displayed transparently on physician profiles prior to confirmation. You agree to settle all out-of-pocket balances, co-pays, or non-covered service fees in accordance with our billing policy.

While MediFlow assists with insurance claim generation, ultimate financial responsibility for services rendered remains with the patient.

7. Acceptable Code of Conduct

Patients and users agree to interact respectfully with all MediFlow physicians, nursing staff, and administrative team members. Abusive language, harassment, fraudulent activity, or misuse of the portal will result in immediate account termination.

8. Limitation of Liability

MediFlow shall not be liable for any indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from technical portal downtime, third-party internet outages, or user failure to follow explicit medical instructions provided by physicians.
