Privacy Policy & Patient Rights

Last updated: August 2026 · Effective for all MediFlow patients, visitors, and digital account holders.

Table of Contents

1. Policy Overview

MediFlow Hospital ("MediFlow," "we," "us," or "our") is committed to maintaining the confidentiality, integrity, and security of your personal information and Protected Health Information (PHI).

This Privacy Policy outlines our clinical data practices across our physical medical facilities, outpatient suites, online booking platform, and patient record portal.

HIPAA & Regulatory Compliance

MediFlow operates in full compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA), federal healthcare privacy laws, and international data protection standards.

2. Protected Health Information (PHI)

Protected Health Information refers to individually identifiable health data created, received, or stored by MediFlow during your care. This includes:

  • Clinical consultation notes, diagnoses, and treatment plans recorded by physicians.
  • Laboratory testing reports, biochemistry panels, and 3D imaging files.
  • E-prescriptions, pharmacy history, and physician discharge summaries.
  • Health insurance policy details, claim history, and billing records.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information directly from you when you register an account, schedule a consultation, visit our clinics, or submit inquiries through our online forms:

A. Personal Identifiers

Full legal name, date of birth, residential address, telephone number, emergency contact details, and government-issued ID numbers.

B. Digital & Portal Data

IP address, browser session identifiers, login credentials, and cookie data strictly required for authenticated session management within the patient portal.

4. How We Use Your Data

MediFlow utilizes collected information strictly for legitimate medical and administrative purposes, including:

  • Coordinating appointment bookings and sending automated SMS/email reminders.
  • Enabling board-certified specialists to evaluate medical history prior to consultations.
  • Processing direct insurance claims and issuing transparent billing invoices.
  • Fulfilling legal and clinical auditing requirements mandated by health authorities.

We do not sell, rent, or trade patient personal data or health records to third-party advertisers under any circumstances.

5. Encryption & Digital Security

MediFlow enforces rigorous technical and administrative safeguards to prevent unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure of patient records:

  • Data Encryption: All portal traffic is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256 encryption.
  • Access Controls: Medical records are accessible only to credentialed physicians and authorized clinic staff directly involved in your treatment.
  • Audit Logs: Every system access request to patient PHI is logged and audited by our compliance security team.

6. Your Patient Rights

Under HIPAA and medical privacy laws, you hold explicit rights regarding your health data:

  • Right to Inspect & Copy: You can request and download a complete digital copy of your medical records and lab reports anytime via the patient portal.
  • Right to Amend: You may request corrections to inaccurate personal or insurance information in your record.
  • Right to Accounting of Disclosures: You may request a log detailing when and why your health information was disclosed to external entities (e.g., insurance claim audits).
